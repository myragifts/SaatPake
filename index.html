Profile Review Notice

Every submitted profile will be reviewed by the SaatPake team. If your profile is approved, we will notify you and share it with suitable matches. If you do not receive a confirmation email from us, your profile may not have been approved.

Submit the registration form only once. Multiple submissions using the same email address or WhatsApp number, without prior permission from the SaatPake team, will result in permanent rejection of the profile.

Your Privacy Matters

  • Contact information is used for registration review and communication.
  • Facebook profile links are used only for internal verification.
  • Contact details are not displayed publicly without permission.
  • Do not submit Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID or identity-document photographs.
  • Do not enter a complete home address.
  • Submitted information must be true and current.
  • SaatPake may reject incomplete, misleading, duplicate or suspicious profiles.
  • Independently verify information before making any personal decision.
  • SaatPake does not guarantee approval, compatibility, marriage or a successful match.
Short Privacy Notice

SaatPake uses submitted details to review registrations, communicate with applicants and privately introduce suitable prospective matches. Private contact and verification information is not displayed publicly without permission.
